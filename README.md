# Chubby Cheeser

Play the live web version directly in your browser here: 
👉 **[Click Here to Play the Game](https://samplesite.fyi/game/chubby-cheeser.html/)**

## How to Play
* **W, A, S, D** or **Arrow Keys**: Move and turn the player.
* **Objective**: Stuff your face with as many cheese wheels as possible! Quickly!!
* **Scoring**: Cheese wheels are 10 points, but every moment of hunger takes away
   from your total score. 
* **Beware**: There are cats hanging around. If they see you scurry by for your favorite
   food, you may just become theirs! Any encounter with a cat will cost you a cheese wheel.
   Get hungry enough and you'll eat some cheese.
* **Game Over**: Run out of cheese and...well...

## How NOT to Play (keyboard required)
* **Mobile Phones**
* **Tablets**
* **Game Consoles**
